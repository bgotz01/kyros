**y = I³**

> Identify the next paradigm.

# Vision

Kyros is an AI-native intelligence platform designed to identify paradigm-defining events and model their long-term impact across markets, industries and society.

Most information is noise.

Some events permanently alter the trajectory of history.

Kyros exists to distinguish the latter from the former.

Rather than asking:

> "What happened?"

Kyros asks:

> "What changes because of this?"

---

# I³

```
y = I³
```

I³ is not a mathematical model.

It is a symbolic equation inspired by the Three Laws of Poesis.

It represents the three stages used to evaluate every significant event.

---

## I¹ — Inversion

**What existing paradigm is being challenged?**

Every new paradigm inverts one or more assumptions of the previous one.

Questions:

- What belief no longer holds and has flipped?
- What constraint has disappeared?
- Which incumbents become vulnerable?

---

## I² — Incentives

**Why will this spread?**

Once a new possibility exists, incentives determine whether it remains a curiosity or reshapes the world.

Questions:

- Who benefits?
- Is adoption economically inevitable?
- What political, technological or social forces accelerate it?

---

## I³ — Inflection

**Is this a genuine turning point?**

Inflection is the moment where the trajectory changes.

Most events are temporary.

Some permanently redirect the future.

Questions:

- Does this represent a new trajectory?
- Is this likely to become historically significant?
- What downstream chains does it initiate?

---

# Philosophy

Kyros does not predict random events.

It evaluates events as they occur.

The objective is not to discover hidden information.

The objective is to correctly interpret the significance of obvious information before consensus fully understands its implications.

History repeatedly demonstrates that paradigm shifts often begin with events that are visible to everyone, but whose long-term consequences are underestimated.

Kyros exists to model those consequences.

---

# Examples

- The release of ChatGPT.
- The publication of *Attention Is All You Need*.
- The first verified footage of a military invasion.
- A breakthrough battery chemistry.
- A landmark regulatory decision.

Each is immediately observable.

The difficult question is not whether it happened.

The difficult question is:

> What changes because of it and how do we position?
