# The Release of ChatGPT

**OpenAI — 30 November 2022.** Not a paper. A product surface placed over an
existing model (GPT-3.5, preference-tuned per InstructGPT).

Reported to have reached roughly 100 million users within two months, among the
fastest consumer adoption curves recorded.

## Inversion

Nothing technical. Every component pre-existed: the architecture (2017), the
scale (2020), the tuning method (March 2022). Kyros should be explicit that the
inversion here is **social, not technical**.

What flipped was the public's model of what software can do. Before, a computer
was something you instructed precisely; after, it was something you asked. The
constraint that disappeared was the requirement that the user know the system's
interface.

## Incentives

The most consequential incentive event in the field's history. It converted a
research programme into a revenue line and a competitive emergency
simultaneously. Google declared an internal code red. Microsoft accelerated its
OpenAI investment. The capex cycle that followed reshaped semiconductor demand,
data-centre construction, and electricity planning in several countries.

It also made AI a political object. Regulation, the EU AI Act timetable, national
compute strategies and export controls all intensified after a consumer product
made the capability legible to legislators.

## Inflection

Unambiguous — and it is the event most often misdated. The technical trajectory
did not change on 30 November 2022. The *attention, capital and political*
trajectories did, all at once.

## Lesson for Kyros

**The distribution event is a separate inflection from the capability event, and
it is the one consensus notices.** Kyros's edge is in the gap between them.

Corollary for scanning: a candidate that adds no capability but removes an access
barrier — an interface, a price cut, an open-weight release, a regulatory
approval — belongs in the same analytical class as a capability paper. Most
tracking systems will not surface it, because it produces no publication.
