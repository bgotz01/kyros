# IBM Watson and Watson Health

**Jeopardy! victory, February 2011. Watson Health launched 2015. MD Anderson
partnership cancelled 2017. Watson Health assets sold to Francisco Partners,
2022.**

## Why it looked like an inflection

- A genuine, unambiguous, televised public demonstration — a machine defeating
  the strongest human players at open-domain question answering.
- Backed by IBM: a century-old firm with enterprise distribution, credibility and
  a large sales force. This is exactly the "incentives and distribution exist"
  condition that Kyros treats as confirming.
- An enormous and obviously valuable target market in clinical decision support.
- Heavy, sustained marketing that made "Watson" briefly synonymous with AI.

## What actually happened

The Jeopardy! system was a heavily engineered, domain-tuned pipeline. Very little
of it generalised. Oncology deployments produced unsafe or unhelpful
recommendations; the MD Anderson programme was cancelled after substantial
expenditure with no clinical deployment. The health division was sold at a
fraction of the capital invested.

## Diagnosis

**Inversion: none, and this is the whole story.** No general constraint was
removed. A specific system was built for a specific, curated, non-adversarial
setting. The demonstration was of engineering, not of a capability that
transferred.

**Incentives: present, and misleading.** IBM had every incentive to push this and
did — with capital, sales and marketing behind it. It still failed, because
incentives can only carry a real inversion. They cannot manufacture one.

**Inflection: none.** Arguably negative: the eventual failure made health systems
more sceptical, raising the barrier for genuinely capable systems arriving later.

## Lesson for Kyros

**The most important negative case in the corpus, because it inverts the usual
warning.** Kyros's standard failure mode is over-weighting technical novelty and
under-weighting distribution. Watson is the opposite error — overwhelming
distribution attached to nothing.

Rules this argues for:
- A demo under curated conditions is evidence about the demo, not the capability.
  Ask what was hand-built for the demonstration and what remains without it.
- Marketing volume is anti-correlated with substance often enough to be treated
  as a mild negative signal.
- **I¹ is a gate, not a weight.** If no assumption is inverted, I² and I³ cannot
  rescue the candidate. Run the stages in order and stop at the first failure.
